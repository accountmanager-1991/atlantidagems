How to use the Instagram templates

  1. Download the SVG (editable) or PNG (quick).
  2. Upload to Canva (or open in Photoshop/Figma).
  3. Delete the dashed placeholder rectangle.
  4. Add your jewelry photo on a layer behind the gold corner brackets.
  5. Crop your photo to a square so it fits inside the frame area.
  6. Export as JPG/PNG and upload to Instagram.

Regenerate any/all templates: node scripts/generate-social-kit.mjs
