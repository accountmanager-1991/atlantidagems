What's new in this redesign

  • 6 KPI cards at top — pending / ready-to-ship / shipped 7d / delivered 30d / revenue 30d / avg order — at-a-glance health check
  • Grid view by default — visual order cards with customer name, country flag, item thumbnails, total, and shipping bar (replaces the dense list)
  • Status-color cards — pending (yellow), paid (cyan), shipped (blue with tracking bar), delivered (green checkmark)
  • Detail panel sticky on the right — click any order, the full info + update form follows you as you scroll
  • "Auto-syncs when you save" panel — see exactly what each Save triggers (GHL contact tag, GHL pipeline stage, customer email, n8n WhatsApp), so you know nothing's missed
  • GHL Pipeline-stage sync (NEW) — moves the lead through your sales pipeline (Lead → Order Placed → Shipped → Delivered) instead of just tagging the contact (requires you to set up a Pipeline in GHL first — I'll show you how if you want)
  • Brand-aligned styling — Cinzel headings, Cormorant body, navy/gold/cream/larimar palette throughout (replacing the gray/blue Tailwind defaults)
